About Auralo V1.0 · Made by ATHRON. Music, beyond the ordinary.
Privacy: songs, artwork, playlists and settings stay in this browser (IndexedDB). Nothing is uploaded and there is no analytics. Clearing site data, private browsing, or removing the browser profile can delete your music, so keep your original files and make backups. YouTube links need an internet connection and play through YouTube.

Backups hold playlists, favorites and YouTube links, not audio files. Shortcuts: Space play/pause · ←/→ seek 5s · ↑/↓ volume · N next · P previous · S shuffle · R repeat · F favorite · M mute · Q queue · / search · Esc close.

Offline audio

Import from your device

Pick audio files you already have (MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG, FLAC). They are saved in this browser and play without internet.

Download for Offline

Only for a direct audio-file link (ending in .mp3, .m4a, .wav, .ogg or .flac) from a source that authorizes downloads, such as your own server or a public-domain or Creative Commons host. Webpages and YouTube links aren’t audio files and can’t be downloaded here. Saved YouTube links stay Online Only.
